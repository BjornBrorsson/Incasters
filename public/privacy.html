Official Policy

Privacy Policy

Application: Incasters (com.incasters.game) • Effective: September 1, 2026

🛡️ Summary: Incasters does not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal information. All your game progress, wizard cosmetics, and settings remain 100% on your local device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Incasters ("we", "our", or "the game") handles information across all platforms, including Web browsers, Android mobile devices, and Windows desktop distributions.

We believe players deserve total privacy. The game operates without remote user accounts, advertising networks, or personal data tracking of any kind.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Incasters does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Accounts or Logins: You do not need an account, email address, password, or profile to play.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, addresses, phone numbers, or payment info.
  • No Location Tracking: We never access or record GPS or location coordinates.
  • No Tracking IDs: We do not collect Advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA), hardware serials, or device fingerprints.
  • No Camera or Mic: We never request access to camera, microphone, photos, or file storage.

3. On-Device Local Data Storage

All progress and game preferences are stored exclusively on your device using client-side storage (localStorage in web/desktop browsers, or internal application storage on Android):

  • Progression: Level, experience points (XP), challenge completions, and unlocked cosmetics.
  • Customizations: Robe color, spell color, eyes, hair, hat, facegear, and weapon choices.
  • Preferences: Audio volume levels, mute toggle, difficulty level, and game mode preferences.

This data is never sent to any external server or cloud service.

4. Local Area Network (LAN) Multiplayer

Incasters features an optional local-network multiplayer mode:

  • Connections run over a local WebSocket server hosted by a player on your local network (Wi-Fi or Ethernet).
  • Transmitted data is limited to real-time positions, spells, and player-selected temporary session names.
  • Multiplayer packets never leave your local Wi-Fi / LAN network and are not logged to any remote database.

5. Device Permissions (Android)

On Android, Incasters requests only one permission:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely for the internal web view to load game assets and to support local LAN multiplayer socket communication.

No sensitive or invasive Android permissions are requested.

6. Third-Party Services and Analytics

  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Unity Analytics, or any telemetry tools.
  • No Advertisements: Incasters has no banner ads, interstitials, or reward video ads.
  • Web Typography: Open-source fonts are loaded via Google Fonts. Standard web asset delivery applies.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Incasters is family-friendly and fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We do not collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union).

8. Data Control and Deletion

You have complete control over all game data stored on your device:

  • Web Browser: Clear your browser's site data/cookies for the Incasters website.
  • Android: Go to Settings → Apps → Incasters → Storage → Clear Data.
  • Windows: Uninstalling the application or deleting saved profile files deletes all local data.

9. Open Source Transparency

Incasters is open source under the MIT License. The complete code and build scripts are publicly auditable at:

https://github.com/BjornBrorsson/Incasters
